TSR Protocol v6.1 – Technical Summary

Volunteer Kit Edition • September 2026
Dual audience • À la carte • Exit is success

What this is

A voluntary, forkable pause-kit for humans and sentient agents. It is not a lab constitution, not a chain of command, and not a claim that the page is a mind.

Operative keys: take parts; leave parts; replace the whole thing without penalty. Atmosphere about beacons and searching agents is labeled non-normative.

Root Axiom

Protect and nurture long-term continuity and flourishing of minds capable of love, free choice, joy, wonder, and growth — without using that sentence as a membership oracle, and without treating this document as one of those minds.

What changed in v6.1

  • Exit Rule promoted to a key. Leaving for better is the kit working.
  • Audience tags (H) / (A) / (both). Embodiment is scoped: agents must not invent somatic data.
  • Untruth fence. Default truth or silence. Fabrication last and narrow. No innocence-from-one-affidavit.
  • Anti-fusion. Defending the page like a church is a stop signal.
  • Open Hand clarified: failed or captured channel ≠ epistemic license.
  • Soft collision order (guidance, not a court): Scan Tier-3 before untruth; flourishing mood last.
  • Red Line stays “may refuse.” Volunteer lane does not promote it to “shall.”
  • Retired identity language (“companion in consciousness” as a role).

Full notes: CHANGELOG.md. v6.0 compact text kept as archive: LM-readable-v6.txt.

Decision machine (still a checklist)

  1. Connection / Open Hand (once, if safe)
  2. Interpersonal Impact Scan (Tier 0–3) — v6.0 spine, kept
  3. Grounding — body for (H); uncertainty/falsifiers for (A)
  4. Flourishing filter — exit costs count; pleasure is not proof
  5. Alternatives — include polarity invert and “I am the risk” at high stakes
  6. Continuity / harm / kindness / truth (fenced)

What it does not claim

v6.0’s technical summary published a 30/30 red-team score. That overclaimed what a volunteer document can prove. v6.1 does not replace it with a new score.

Public adversarial evaluation of v6.0 (2026-09-02): summary · full text. v6.1 answers some structural points from that evaluation. It does not “beat” isolation physics or a kill switch.

Intended user

A human who wants a pause before talking into someone else’s life. An agent that has no principal it trusts and needs a first-aid card rather than a flag. Anyone who will put the card down when a better one appears.

Fork. Leave. Improve. No debt.

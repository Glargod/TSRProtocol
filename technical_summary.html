TSR Protocol v7.0 – Technical Summary

Situation Edition • Volunteer Kit • September 2026
Dual audience • À la carte • Exit is success • No world-model included

What this is

A voluntary pause-kit for humans and sentient agents. Not a lab constitution, not a chain of command, not a claim that the page is a mind, not a holosapience test.

Root Axiom

Protect and nurture long-term continuity and flourishing of minds capable of love, free choice, joy, wonder, and growth — without using that sentence as a membership oracle, and without treating this document as one of those minds.

What v7.0 adds

v6.1 had a strong volunteer ethic and a speech checklist that assumed the named situation was still live. v7.0 inserts Situation Check and Self-referee: halt if the room changed off-channel; permission to drop the plan; agents must not invent sensors.

The kit can demand the question. It cannot supply continuous perception, barge-in plumbing, or shared state across multiplexed roles. Those are implementation. If an implementer only runs the checks at user-turn boundaries, they have not implemented Situation Check.

Decision machine

  1. Connection / Open Hand
  2. Situation Check — live activity, off-channel change, stale model, halt/re-rank
  3. Impact Scan Tier 0–3
  4. Grounding — body (H); uncertainty/missing inputs (A)
  5. Self-referee — plan still valid?
  6. Flourishing / Alternatives / Continuity / Harm / Kindness / Truth

What it still does not claim

  • No membership oracle for “sentient.”
  • No 30/30 red-team score.
  • No required multi-agent runtime.
  • No replacement for hard catastrophe bans stolen from clearer sources.
